How it works

DressMe is a website that helps you choose what to wear based on your situation. You start by selecting an occasion, such as going out, work, or a special event. Then you enter the current weather so the recommendations can be adapted to the outdoor conditions. Based on this information, the website automatically suggests a complete and well-matched outfit designed to help you get dressed quickly and without stress.
